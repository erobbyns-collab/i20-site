As parties engage in a war of words, the electorate is left feeling caught in the crossfire, questioning which party can genuinely alleviate their struggles. A decline in trust towards Westminster can lead to disengagement from the political process, as citizens lose faith in their ability to influence change. Voter turnout may decrease, and civic participation could dwindle, compounding the existing issues of political apathy and disillusionment. For a healthy democracy to thrive, an engaged and informed electorate is essential. Thus, it is imperative that politicians and policymakers re-establish a connection with the public, demonstrating a genuine commitment to addressing the pressing economic issues reflected in statistics like the CPIH.
