The 1970s stand as a notable example, when rampant inflation contributed to significant political upheaval. Today's political landscape, marked by the repercussions of Brexit and the ongoing challenges of a post-pandemic recovery, seems eerily reminiscent. Amidst these dynamics, the challenge for Westminster lies in rebuilding trust through transparency and effective policy-making. How the government addresses inflation, particularly regarding essential services and cost-of-living support, will be crucial in defining its legacy.
