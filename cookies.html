Cookie Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help site operators understand how visitors use their site.

Cookies We Use

i20 does not set any first-party cookies. The only cookies on this site are set by Google Analytics, which we use to understand readership patterns.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Maintains session state 2 years

How to Manage Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that blocking cookies will not affect your ability to read i20 content.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

No Advertising or Tracking Cookies

i20 does not run any advertising. We do not use marketing cookies, retargeting pixels, or social media tracking scripts.

Changes to This Policy

If we introduce additional cookies in the future, we will update this page accordingly.
